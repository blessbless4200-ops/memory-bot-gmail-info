Memory Bot Gmail is a privately operated assistant that helps its owner search and read Gmail messages and send email when explicitly instructed.

这是供所有者个人使用的邮箱助手：按指令查询和读取邮件，并在获得明确授权后发送邮件。本页面用于介绍应用及其数据处理方式，不提供公众注册。

This is an information website. It does not ask for your Gmail password, collect email messages, or provide an OAuth sign-in form.

What the assistant does

  • Searches messages relevant to the owner's request.
  • Reads selected messages or conversation threads to help answer questions and prepare follow-ups.
  • Sends an email only after the owner authorizes its recipient and message.

Gmail permissions

gmail.readonly enables message searches and reading. This is a mailbox-level permission; a search filter is not a technical restriction to just one thread.

gmail.send enables sending. The integration does not request Gmail modification or deletion permissions. Basic Google account identification scopes are also used to identify the authorized account.

You remain in control

You can revoke access in your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not automatically erase earlier conversation records or files. See the privacy notice for details.
