Privacy notice

Effective: 31 October 2026 · Applies to the Memory Bot Gmail integration.

本说明针对私人邮箱助手，而非 KILLWINNER 商城的购物业务。邮箱数据不会在此说明网站上公开展示。

1. Operator and purpose

Memory Bot Gmail is operated for its owner's personal use. Privacy questions and deletion requests can be directed to blessbless4200@gmail.com. This notice covers access to Google account and Gmail data by the assistant.

2. Data accessed

The integration uses Google account identifiers, an OAuth authorization grant, and Gmail data needed for the requested task. Gmail data may include message and thread identifiers, sender and recipient addresses, subjects, dates, labels, and message bodies. Sending uses the recipient, subject, body and reply reference supplied or approved by the owner.

The granted Gmail permissions are gmail.readonly and gmail.send. Read access technically covers the authorized mailbox; the assistant is instructed to limit actual queries and reads to the owner's task.

3. Use and processing

Data is used to search, read, summarize and help respond to email, and to send explicitly authorized messages. Relevant message content and tool results may enter the configured AI agent's context and conversation records. Processing is not limited to the owner's computer.

The workflow uses Google's Gmail API, the separately hosted Memory Bot runtime, and the configured AI model and messaging services involved in the owner's interaction. Data provided to these services is subject to the applicable service settings and data handling terms. Sending email discloses its content and addressing information to the specified recipients and mail providers.

Permitted uses exclude selling Gmail data, advertising targeting, or using Gmail-derived data to develop or train generalized AI models. Memory Bot Gmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage and protection

OAuth credentials are kept in the Memory Bot integration's private encrypted credential storage. Credentials are not part of this public website. Relevant email excerpts, generated drafts, files and interaction history may persist in the agent runtime or conversation services. This notice does not promise zero retention, fully local processing, or end-to-end encryption across the entire workflow.

5. Retention and deletion

Authorization credentials remain until removed, replaced or invalidated. Task records and generated files may remain until the owner deletes them or the relevant service applies its retention policy; this integration does not impose a single automatic deletion period across all services. Backups and service logs may follow separate retention schedules.

The owner may request review or deletion of retained integration credentials and task data through the contact address above. Copies held by message recipients and independently operated services may require separate deletion actions.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. This stops future use of the authorization. Revocation alone does not erase previously processed data.

7. This public website

These static pages do not provide an email upload form or mailbox access. The hosting and delivery services may process routine connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to serve and protect the pages.

8. Changes

Update this notice when the integration's permissions or data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.
